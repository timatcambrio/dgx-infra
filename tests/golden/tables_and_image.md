## Booking Policy

<!-- dgx:block=tables-and-image:p000:b004 -->
Travel costs are reimbursed at the rates in effect on the date the travel began. Receipts are required for any single expense of twenty-five dollars or more. Requests submitted more than sixty days after the end of travel require written justification from the department head.

<!-- dgx:block=tables-and-image:p000:b005 -->
- Reserve at least 24 hours ahead
- Cancel unused holds by 9am so others can book
- Report AV issues to facilities, not the room's occupant

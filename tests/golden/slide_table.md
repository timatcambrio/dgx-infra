# How The Limit Is Set

<!-- dgx:block=slide-table:p002:b002 -->
Account holders may contribute up to the annual maximum set by the Internal Revenue Service. The limit depends on whether the account holder is enrolled in single coverage or in family coverage, and it is published again each calendar year. Contributions made after the end of a year but before the filing deadline may be applied to the earlier year.
